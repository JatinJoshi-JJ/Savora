Step by Step

The iconic 6-stage stovetop technique.

1

BOIL WATER

⏱️ 2 Mins

Take 2 cups (approx. 300 ml per noodle cake) of fresh filtered water in a saucepan and bring it to a rolling boil.

2

ADD NOODLES

⏱️ 30 Secs

Add the noodle cake and break gently in half if you prefer manageable strands. Toss in diced veggies.

3

ADD TASTEMAKER

⏱️ 30 Secs

Tear open the masala tastemaker sachet and sprinkle evenly across the water. Stir gently to disperse spices.

4

COOK ON MEDIUM FLAME

⏱️ 2 Mins

Cook for 2 minutes on medium flame, stirring occasionally so the noodles absorb the spiced reduction without sticking.

5

CHECK & STIR

⏱️ 30 Secs

Check noodle tenderness. Add 1 tbsp butter for a silky sheen. If you prefer less broth, simmer 30 seconds more.

6

SERVE HOT

Instant

Pour into deep ceramic bowls. Garnish with torn fresh basil and cilantro. Enjoy while piping hot!

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick kitchen troubleshooting for the ultimate noodle bowl.

Adding exactly 300 ml per cake allows wheat starches to emulsify with the tastemaker spices into a glossy, velvety reduction without leaving the bowl soupy or dry.

Keep the heat on medium-high and turn off the flame right when 2 tablespoons of liquid remain. The noodles keep cooking from residual heat while you plate them.

Yes! Simply replace the salted butter with cold-pressed coconut oil, toasted sesame oil, or extra virgin olive oil. The noodle cakes and tastemaker spices are plant-based and contain no dairy or animal extracts.

Diced bell peppers, sweet golden corn, baby spinach, button mushrooms, and pan-seared paneer or tofu work wonderfully. Sauté crunchy vegetables for 45 seconds in light butter before adding the noodles to preserve color and crisp bite.

Stir in a single slice of processed cheddar cheese or 1 tablespoon of fresh cream during the final 30 seconds of simmering. The heat seamlessly melts the cheese into the spice broth, creating a decadent, glossy sauce.
